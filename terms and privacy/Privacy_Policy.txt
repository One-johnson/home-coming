This Privacy Policy explains how Loyalty House International (“Loyalty House,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, 
retains, and protects personal data in connection with homecomingconvention.com (the “Site”) and the Homecoming 
Convention (the “Event”). It is intended to reflect Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), including its principles of 
accountability, lawful and reasonable processing, purpose specification, data quality, openness, security safeguards, and data-
subject participation. 
1. Data controller and contact 
The UD is the data controller for the personal data described in this Policy, except where a third party independently determines 
how it uses your data. 
2. Scope 
This Policy applies when you visit the Site, register yourself or others, purchase accommodation, contact us, receive Event 
communications, or attend the Event. It does not govern a hotel, payment processor, social network, transport provider, or 
other third party acting as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. 
3. Personal data we collect 
• Identity and contact data: name, title, age or date of birth where needed, gender where relevant to rooming, country, city, 
organization or church, role, email address, telephone number, and emergency-contact information. 
• Registration and group data: attendee type, registration selections, group affiliation, coordinator details, attendance 
history, badge information, and communications or requests. 
• Accommodation and travel data: occupant and roommate names, room preferences, arrival/departure dates, flight or 
transport details if provided, accessibility requests, and information needed to coordinate lodging. 
• Transaction data: items purchased, amount, currency, payment status, order number, receipts, refund or dispute 
information, and limited payment metadata. Full card credentials are ordinarily collected directly by the payment 
processor, not by us. 
• Sensitive or special personal data: religious affiliation or ministry role, dietary information that may reveal religious beliefs, 
and accessibility or health-related information you choose or need to provide. We ask that you provide only what is 
necessary. 
• Technical and usage data: IP address, browser and device information, referring pages, pages viewed, timestamps, 
approximate location derived from IP, cookies, and similar identifiers. 
• Communications and media: emails, forms, survey responses, support requests, photographs, video, audio, livestream 
footage, and testimonials where applicable. 
4. How we collect data 
• Directly from you when you submit a form, make a payment, contact us, or attend the Event. 
• From a group coordinator, pastor, family member, roommate, or purchaser who registers or pays for you. 
• Automatically through the Site, server logs, cookies, analytics, and similar technology. 
• From service providers such as payment processors, hotels, registration platforms, email providers, security providers, and 
event contractors. 
• From publicly available or ministry sources where lawful and relevant to Event administration.

5. Why we use personal data 
• To register attendees, process purchases and refunds, issue confirmations and badges, and maintain transaction records. 
• To arrange rooms, roommates, transport or arrival coordination, meals, accessibility support, safeguarding, and other 
Event logistics. 
• To communicate essential information, changes, reminders, safety notices, and customer support responses. 
• To prevent fraud, secure the Site, investigate disputes, enforce our Terms, protect people and property, and comply with 
law. 
• To operate, troubleshoot, measure, and improve the Site and Event. 
• With appropriate permission or another lawful basis, to send ministry or future-event marketing and use testimonials or 
identifiable promotional media. 
• To create attendance, financial, planning, and ministry reports, including aggregated or de-identified statistics where 
feasible. 
6. Basis for processing 
We process personal data when necessary to perform or take steps related to your registration or accommodation contract; 
when you have consented; when processing is required or authorized by law; when necessary to protect a vital interest; or when 
necessary for our legitimate interests or those of a third party, provided those interests do not unfairly override your rights. 
Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it prospectively. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully 
completed and may mean we cannot provide an optional feature. Some information is mandatory for registration or 
accommodation; we will identify it where practicable. 
7. Special personal data and religious context 
Because the Event is organized by a religious organization, registration may reveal religious beliefs, church affiliation, or ministry 
role. We process such information only where it is necessary for legitimate religious or Event purposes and permitted by law, or 
with consent where required. 
Health, accessibility, allergy, and dietary information is restricted to people and providers who reasonably need it to support 
your request, safety, or Event arrangements. Do not submit detailed medical records through a general registration form. 
8. Payments 
Payment details are submitted through the payment provider displayed at checkout, which may include Paystack or another 
provider. The provider may collect card, bank, mobile-money, identity, device, fraud-prevention, and transaction information 
under its own privacy policy. We receive transaction status, reference, amount, payer details, and other information needed to 
reconcile payments, issue receipts, handle refunds, and address disputes. 
We do not intentionally store full card numbers or card security codes on our systems. Do not send payment-card details by 
email, messaging app, or an unsecured form. 
9. Cookies and analytics 
The Site may use strictly necessary cookies for security, checkout, session management, and core functions. With any consent 
required by applicable law, we may also use analytics or preference cookies to understand Site use and improve performance. 
You can manage cookies through the Site’s consent tool, if available, and your browser. Blocking necessary cookies may 
prevent registration or payment functions from working. Before launch, the cookie banner and this section should identify the 
actual analytics and marketing tools in use. 
Analytics/advertising tools actually used: NONE

• Payment processors, banks, and fraud-prevention providers for transactions and disputes. 
• Hotels, venues, caterers, transport providers, badge printers, and other Event suppliers, limited to information needed to 
provide the service.

11. International transfers 
The Event may involve attendees, hotels, ministry offices, and technology providers in multiple countries. Your personal data

use reasonable contractual, organizational, and technical safeguards and limit transfers to what is necessary. For personal 
data originating in another jurisdiction, additional protections may apply. 
12. Retention 
We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including Event delivery, 
accounting, tax, audit, safeguarding, dispute resolution, legal compliance, and ministry administration. We then delete, 
anonymize, or securely archive it as appropriate.

13. Security and data breaches 
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal data against loss, 
unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot 
guarantee absolute security. 
If a security breach affects personal data, we will investigate and provide notices to the Data Protection Commission and 
affected individuals as required by applicable law.

information about recipients; ask us to correct inaccurate, incomplete, excessive, irrelevant, outdated, or misleading data; 
request deletion where we no longer have authority to retain it; object to certain processing; prevent direct marketing; or raise a 
concern about processing likely to cause unwarranted damage or distress.

15. Marketing communications 
Where permitted, we may send news about future conventions, ministry activities, or related opportunities. You may 
unsubscribe using the link in a marketing email or by contacting us. You will still receive transactional and essential Event 
communications relating to a current booking.

minor’s registration and only provide data necessary for attendance, safeguarding, and accommodation. We may seek 
additional consent or verification for photographs, sensitive information, or other processing involving minors.
